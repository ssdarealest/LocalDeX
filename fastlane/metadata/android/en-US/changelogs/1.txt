Initial FOSS release of LocalDeX v1.0.0:
- Native loopback ADB TLS pairing and connection (Android 11+).
- Scrcpy-server v3.1 integration for virtual display creation.
- Material 3 Expressive UI with DeX Cobalt theme and OLED dark mode.
- Galaxy Z Fold posture awareness (tabletop mode, cover/inner screen profiles).
- Low-latency MediaCodec video pipeline (H.264 / H.265).
- Multi-mode input engine (Virtual Trackpad, Direct Touch, hardware mouse & keyboard).
- Self-healing WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS grant.
